Tuteliq is a child-safety deep tech company. It researches, trains and runs its own proprietary models rather than wrapping someone else's, develops them with criminologists and child-protection specialists, and works inside the child-protection community, connected to the organisations that lead the field.

## Internet Watch Foundation member

Tuteliq became a member of the [Internet Watch Foundation](https://www.iwf.org.uk/membership/our-members/tuteliq/) (IWF) on 1 June 2026. The IWF is the UK's leading organisation for finding and removing child sexual abuse material online, and a globally recognised authority in the field.

Tuteliq contributes behavioural detection expertise: reading how a conversation develops over time to identify grooming, exploitation and coercion at the earliest stage. Read the [IWF announcement](https://www.iwf.org.uk/news-media/news/new-partnership-aims-to-boost-early-detection-of-online-grooming-and-child-sexual-abuse-material).

For customers who hold their own IWF membership, Tuteliq can apply the IWF Hash List to their image checks. It is applied only once that membership has been confirmed, as the IWF Member Licence requires.

> "Protecting children online requires more than identifying harmful content after it has been shared. By analysing how conversations evolve over time rather than scanning individual messages, Tuteliq's technology identifies grooming, exploitation and coercive behaviour at the earliest possible stage before a child is harmed."
>
> Dr. Nicola Harding, Chief Scientific Officer, Tuteliq

## International Justice Mission technical white paper

Tuteliq contributed to *Tech that Protects: Building Smart Devices that Can't Create or Display Child Sexual Abuse Images and Videos*, a technical feasibility assessment facilitated by International Justice Mission's Centre to End Online Sexual Exploitation of Children. The paper examines how smart devices could be designed so they cannot be used to create or display child sexual abuse material, with on-device safeguards that preserve privacy and end-to-end encryption.

Tuteliq published a dedicated **Tuteliq Technical Brief** alongside the paper, on on-device and privacy-preserving detection. Both are on the [IJM white paper page](https://www.ijmuk.org/technicalwhitepaper).

> "I'm convinced we don't have to choose between protecting children and protecting privacy. With careful, privacy-preserving design, such as detection on the device, no data retention and transparent, independently tested systems, we can do both."
>
> Dr. Gabriel Sabadin, Founder, Tuteliq AB

## Built with criminologists

Tuteliq's detection is behavioural, not keyword-based: it reads intent, context and how a conversation escalates. The models are developed under the scientific lead of **Dr. Nicola Harding**, criminologist and Chief Scientific Officer, and informed by criminological research on grooming, coercive control and behavioural escalation.
