## The problem

Child safety compliance shouldn't require a machine learning team. The Kids Online Safety Act (KOSA), pending US federal legislation, would require covered platforms to protect minors from nine categories of harm: bullying, grooming, eating disorders, substance use, self-harm, depression, compulsive usage, sexual exploitation, and unsafe visual content. Building detection for even one of these categories takes months of ML engineering, training data, and ongoing maintenance. Building for all nine, across text, voice, and images, with age-appropriate calibration? That's a team-year of work.

Tuteliq does it in a single API call.
